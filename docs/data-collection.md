_Owner: BUILD_TRAINER (section 11A). This is the physical operator protocol the
team follows every collection session. Filled in BUILD_TRAINER._

## Mic placement

_To be written in BUILD_TRAINER: exact mic position and geometry, kept identical
across every session (one mic for everything)._

## Key-press technique

_To be written in BUILD_TRAINER: paced cadence (TRAINER_PACED_GAP_MS), striking
technique, consistency._

## Environment

_To be written in BUILD_TRAINER: quiet room, OS audio processing off (no echo
cancellation, noise suppression, or auto gain)._

## Per-typist and multi-keyboard workflow

_To be written in BUILD_TRAINER: collect from 2 to 3 typists (TRAINER_MIN_TYPISTS)
on both the blue and C3 Equalz boards, all through the one mic._

## The three sessions

_To be written in BUILD_TRAINER: Session A (train), Session B (held-out eval,
ideally a different typist), and the demo session, and why eval is a SEPARATE
